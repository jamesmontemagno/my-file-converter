LocalMorph

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Convert videos, audio, and images on your PC. Batch files, trim clips, choose a sharing preset, and keep your files on your device.

DESCRIPTION
Your files. Your computer. The format you need.

LocalMorph is a desktop file converter that processes your files locally, without uploading them to a conversion server. Turn a video into a shareable MP4, extract its audio, convert a batch of photos, or prepare clips for your next project.

START SIMPLE, GO DEEPER WHEN YOU NEED TO
Add files or folders, choose a quick preset or output format, and review the batch before converting. See which files are compatible with your settings. Preview images and video in an expandable inspector, with a settings panel that grows with your window.

VIDEO, AUDIO, AND IMAGES
Work with formats including MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, Opus, PNG, JPEG, and WebP. Choose quality, resolution, frame rate, and audio settings. Hardware video encoding is used when supported and verified on your PC. Format availability depends on the selected conversion engine and installed components.

TOOLS FOR REAL MEDIA WORK
Trim clips, grab a frame, extract audio, select audio tracks, or add subtitles. Use a two-pass target-size preset to prepare files for sharing. Join compatible video clips without re-encoding, or split a video into shorter clips. Accurate splitting re-encodes; fast stream-copy splitting follows source keyframes. Optional measured loudness normalization, audio silence trimming, and supported HDR-to-SDR tone mapping provide more control when you need it.

A QUEUE YOU CAN FOLLOW
Import up to 2,000 supported files at a time. Convert in parallel, follow progress, and cancel individual jobs or the batch. Review completed files in history and reopen their results. Existing source files are protected, and removing a history entry does not delete the converted file.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
Media conversion runs on your PC. The Microsoft Store edition includes FFmpeg and ffprobe for video and audio processing. Document conversion and some image formats require optional external tools or Windows codecs; the Tools page shows what is available and what is missing. Installing optional tools or codecs may require internet access and acceptance of their own licenses.
