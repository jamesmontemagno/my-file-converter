The workflow retains the exact upstream source archive, builder, configure flags,

Rebuild on Apple Silicon macOS with the recorded Xcode version:

  bash build-proof-media.sh ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz new-output-directory arm64

GPL, nonfree, network and automatic external-library discovery are disabled.
Apple system frameworks are not included in the source archive.

FFmpeg is statically linked with libass 0.17.4 (ISC), FreeType 2.13.3 (FreeType
License), FriBidi 1.0.16 (LGPL-2.1+), HarfBuzz 11.2.1 (MIT) and zimg 3.0.6 (WTFPL)
for subtitle burn-in and HDR tone mapping; their licenses are in licenses/ and
their exact source archives are retained beside the package. Subtitles render with
macOS system fonts through CoreText. No optional external engines are included;
installing host tools does not enable missing features. Full shipping-component and distribution review remains
a separate prerequisite before App Store review and public release.
